"Souls on the Banks of the Acheron" refers to a famous 1898 painting by Adolf Hirémy-Hirschl, depicting naked souls awaiting guidance from Hermes (the psychopomp) to cross the River Acheron into the underworld, a scene from Greek mythology showing raw human emotion, mortality, and the transition to the afterlife, with Charon visible in the distance.
